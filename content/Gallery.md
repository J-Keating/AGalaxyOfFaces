# Gallery

This file controls the gallery introduction, photo order, captions, and accessible image descriptions. Add, remove, or reorder complete example blocks. Use paths relative to `index.html`.

Before publishing a recognizable child, confirm that a parent or guardian has approved public website use. Do not put a child's name in a filename, caption, or alt text.

## Introduction

- **Eyebrow:** A few favourites
- **Headline:** Every face becomes a new story.
- **Description:** From quick festival designs to special birthday requests, each look is adapted to the person in the chair.
