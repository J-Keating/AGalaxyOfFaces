## Biography

Hi, I'm Macy, the face painter behind A Galaxy of Faces. I love turning a favourite animal, superhero, flower, or completely original idea into something a child can proudly wear.

I create a relaxed, friendly experience and use professional face paints with clean tools and careful hygiene practices.
